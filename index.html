Our Story

Small Herd. Big Heart.

Fresco Dairy Farms started with a simple idea: raise a few cows well, rather than many cows carelessly. Every animal on our farm is known by name, hand-fed and cared for daily — and that care shows up in the quality of the milk, paneer, ghee and curd we produce.

We're a small operation today, and growing steadily — always keeping the same hands-on approach as we add to our herd.
